Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

1. Introduction

Kodak Logistics ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over it, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

2. Information We Collect

When you book storage, request a return, or leave a review, we collect only what's needed to provide the service:

  • Contact details: full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Location details: your hostel name or pickup/delivery location.
  • Booking details: items stored, quantities, pickup/return dates and times, and special instructions you provide.
  • Payment information: your chosen payment method and, for Mobile Money payments, the transaction ID you provide to verify payment. We do not collect or store your Mobile Money PIN or card details.
  • Reviews: your name and any comments or ratings you choose to submit.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and confirm your storage and return bookings.
  • Coordinate pickup and delivery with our team.
  • Verify mobile money payments you report to us.
  • Respond to questions or support requests you send us.
  • Display customer reviews on our website (only with your submission and consent).
  • Improve our service based on booking and feedback trends.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information where necessary to operate our service:

  • With mobile money providers, solely to verify payments you make to us.
  • With our own staff, on a need-to-know basis, to carry out pickups and deliveries.
  • Where required by Ghanaian law or a valid legal request.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored on secure, access-controlled servers. Administrator accounts are protected by hashed passwords and authenticated access, and only authorized staff can view booking and customer records.

6. Data Retention

We retain booking and customer information for as long as necessary to provide our service, resolve disputes, and meet any legal or accounting obligations. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time, subject to Section 7.

7. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, where it is no longer needed for an active booking or legal obligation.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the footer below.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

Our public website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Our admin dashboard uses browser local storage to keep an administrator signed in between visits — this is used only for authentication and is not shared with third parties.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for university students and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, reach us via WhatsApp or email using the contact details in the footer below.
