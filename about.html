About Kodak Logistics

Ghana's student-focused storage and pickup service — built to make vacation move-outs simple.

Who We Are

Kodak Logistics is a Ghana-based storage and logistics service built for university students. Every semester break, students across the country face the same problem: too much to carry home, nowhere safe to leave it, and no easy way to get it back when school resumes. We built Kodak Logistics to solve exactly that.

We collect items directly from your hostel, store them securely for the vacation period, and deliver them back to you when you return — no stress, no lugging bags across the country.

What We Do

Our service covers the full cycle of student storage:

  • Free hostel pickup — we come to you, nationwide, at a date and time that works for your schedule.
  • Secure storage — your items are kept in a monitored facility for the duration of your break.
  • Return delivery — request your items back with 24 hours' notice and we'll deliver them to your hostel.

We store everything from bags and suitcases to fridges, microwaves, gas cylinders, storage containers and televisions — priced by item and size, with no hidden fees.
